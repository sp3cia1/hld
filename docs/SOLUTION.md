# Solution

Update this whenever a decision is settled. Record decisions, not a diary. For a big decision, link to `../decisions/` (outside the repo) instead of retelling it.

## The approach

At startup the app reads the catalog into memory.

**Building a package:**

1. The builder tries every city plan (one, two or three cities): each order of distinct cities in the country, linked one to the next, at least 2 nights each, adding up to the trip.
2. For each plan it builds the cheapest valid package: flights, hotels with the fewest rooms that fit, transfers, and one attraction on each full day. Where parts depend on each other (flight, hotel and airport transfer), it searches the combinations rather than picking each part alone.
3. The cheapest total within budget wins. If none is within budget, the cheapest overall is shown, labelled as over budget.

**Checking it:** a validator, separate from the builder, checks every rule in §4 of the statement. It runs on every built package, after every change and before every export.

**Changing it:** every change follows one path. Apply it to a copy, repair what the app chose (how and when), validate, then save it or refuse with the reason ([0011](../../decisions/0011-repair-how-and-when-never-what.md)).

**How the builder works, step by step:** `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**Keeping it:** Postgres stores packages and confirmed reservations (a full snapshot), so both survive a page refresh and a backend restart.

## Scope and order

1. **Walking skeleton:** form → build one package → day-by-day plan → confirm → reopen by number. Tag `demo-1`.
2. **M6:** a change goes copy → pick the airport transfers it affects again → validate → save, or refuse with the reasons. Hotels and flights are built; attraction changes come next.
3. **The rest of the Musts:** M1 messages, M5 total, budget and export, M8 over budget.
4. **Good to have:** G1 (fill the repair step), G2 price differences, G4 three packages, G3 change cities.
5. **Proposed edges (confirm with Saumya):** "never a dead end" and "see what changed" (`IDEAS.md`).
6. **Then `IDEAS.md`,** by demo impact ÷ effort.

## Basics

Every team will have these, so we need them too (`docs/SLOP.md`):

- A rules panel on the package screen that shows the rules passing.
- A fuzz test: random requests through build, export and validate, checking that the total equals the sum of the parts.
- All rule numbers (60, 180, night limits) in one place, for the live code change.
- The restart moment in the demo: confirm, restart the backend, reopen the reservation.
- The frontend builds against a saved example package until the engine is ready.
- **Our own basic:** the overnight departure made obvious ("You leave Bengaluru on Fri 9 Apr at 23:50, the evening before your holiday starts"), with local times, the time zone, and "+1" where the date changes.

## Built

- **Catalog loader:** reads the 8 files at startup, checks every record and every reference between files, and builds the indexes. The API stops with a clear message if the data doesn't match.
- **`POST /api/packages`, the builder:**
  - checks the request against the data (M1);
  - builds the cheapest valid package (M2, M3);
  - returns `OK`, `OVER_BUDGET` (saved and labelled), or `NO_PACKAGE` with the reason (nothing saved);
  - saves packages to Postgres.

  Steps: `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

- **`GET /api/options`:** the departure cities and countries for the form, from `destinations.json` (M1).
- **Request form (`/`):** the 7 fields. Shape errors show on the field before sending; the API's checks against the
  data (nights, dates) come back as field messages too. `NO_PACKAGE` shows its reason under the form, with the
  values kept for editing.
- **Package page (`/packages/:id`):** the over-budget label (M8), total,
  budget and amount left or over (M5), and the day-by-day plan (M4).
  Packages are persisted in PostgreSQL and retrieved through
  `GET /api/packages/:id`. The page supports refreshes, direct navigation,
  and shared links, including after a backend restart.

- **Free time on the arrival and change days** (`logic/free-time.logic.ts`, `free-time-list.tsx`): the day's card shows when the
  family reaches the hotel and the attractions that still fit, with times and prices. It isn't part of the package or
  the export. Adding one comes with attraction changes (M6). City-change days get the same list (reach the hotel =
  train arrival + the taxi from the station).
- **Changing the hotel or a flight (M6, `apps/api/src/modules/package-changes`):**
  - `GET /api/packages/:id/choices/hotel?city=…` and `GET /api/packages/:id/choices/flight?direction=arrival|return`
    list the choices, each with its price and a dry-run preview: READY with the difference on the total, or BLOCKED
    with the reason (`choices.logic.ts`).
  - `POST /api/packages/:id/changes` applies one change on a copy: swap the item, pick the affected airport transfers
    again, run the validator, then save and answer `APPLIED` with the package, or answer `REFUSED` with every reason
    and save nothing (`change.logic.ts`, `tryChange`). Over budget is applied and labelled.
  - The package page has a Change button on each hotel and flight. The panel lists the choices (5 shown, the rest
    behind "Show more") with a Current badge, the difference on the total and, for blocked ones, the reason. An
    applied change updates the plan and the budget at once; a refused one shows its reasons in the panel.
  - Attraction changes slot in as more types in `changeRequestSchema` (`packages/shared/src/package-change.ts`), with
    one apply function each beside `changeHotel` and `changeFlight`.
- **Validator (`validator.logic.ts`):** `validateTrip(trip, catalog)` returns every broken §4 rule as
  `{ rule, itemId, message }`, and `checkTrip` adds a pass/fail line per rule for the rules panel. The 12 rules and
  their titles are in `packages/shared/src/trip-check.ts`. Written from the statement, not the builder, and imports
  none of the builder's helpers, so it is a real second check. It already covers city changes (one link transfer on
  its timetable; a visit that day needs the station taxi), ready for the builder's several cities.

## Cut

- **Station taxis in the first package.** The FAQ says they aren't needed. They're added only when the traveler adds a visit on a change day.
- **Attractions on the arrival and city-change days in the first package.** They're optional under §4. The page
  lists what still fits instead (Built).
- **Changing a reservation after confirm.**
- **The data ingest pipeline.** The catalog is read into memory instead.

## Proof

Numbers and checks that show it works.

- **Menon:** the builder returns exactly the hand-checked package in `logic/menon-example.ts`: Tokyo, ₹2,19,324 of ₹3,50,000. A unit test checks it.
- **Independent rule check (13 Nov):** 2,221 packages from the builder, checked against every §4 rule by a separate script written from the rules text. **0 problems.** The packages covered every departure city, both countries, every start date and trip length, and 4 kinds of party: 1 adult; 2 adults + 1 child; 4 adults + 3 children with a 17-year-old; 3 adults + an infant.
- **Several cities (13 Nov):** 2,615 packages from 4,320 requests (5 party types) checked by a separate §4 script: **0 problems**. Totals equal a separate prototype. Versus one city: none lost, none dearer, 1,085 cheaper, 222 new.
- **Validator in the test suite:** `validator.logic.test.ts` builds every request for 3 parties (1,867 packages)
  and validates each: 0 problems. It also breaks the Menon package 12 ways (a pickup too soon after landing, a full
  day with no visit, a closed weekday, a twin room for a family…) and each is caught under the right rule. A
  hand-built Tokyo → Kyoto trip passes, and a train off its timetable, a missing train and a visit without the
  station taxi are caught.
- **Attraction assignment:** the Hungarian algorithm gives the same optimum as trying every assignment, on 3,000 random cases.
- **Free time (13 Nov):** every suggestion for the packages (all requests, 5 kinds of party) checked by a separate
  script written from §4: in the city of that night's hotel, open that day, not on a closed date, starting once the
  family reaches the hotel, ending by closing, not already in the package, right price. 25,080 suggestions, including 1,333 on change days, **0 problems**.
- **Changes (M6, 13 Nov):**
  - `change.logic.test.ts`: Menon with `FL-405` gets the Narita taxi to Ueno instead of the bus (+₹4,620: seats
    −₹3,780, taxi +₹8,400) and passes the validator; `FL-740` and `FL-411` are refused with the validator's reason; the
    Asakusa family room picks both airport transfers again for Asakusa; a room too small and a room full on 12 Apr
    are refused under `rooms`. In Delhi, 1 Apr, 5 nights (Tokyo, then Osaka), a hotel change in Tokyo picks only the
    arrival transfer again and one in Osaka only the return transfer.
  - `choices.logic.test.ts`: the Menon flight lists (`FL-403` hidden for 2 seats, `FL-740` and `FL-411` blocked), the
    Tokyo hotel list (3 single rooms hidden, Ginza Pearl blocked), and every READY preview equal to the real change.
  - `package-changes.test.ts`, on the test database: the choices, an applied and a refused change, over budget
    labelled, the 400s, 404 and both 409s. The lost-update test fails when the `updatedAt` condition is removed.

    before and after on 8,640 requests (6,128 packages, 5 parties, two budgets).
  - Browser: the Menon path (`FL-405` at +₹4,620 brings the taxi, `FL-740` refused with its reason, a hotel change
    updates the budget) and the over-budget message after a change and after a refresh.
